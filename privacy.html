shorts-factory (네모연구소 · SquareLab) — Last updated: 2026-10-20

English

shorts-factory is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to, and read analytics from, the owner's own YouTube channels (네모연구소 and SquareLab). It accesses YouTube data only through the YouTube API Services after the owner grants permission with Google OAuth.

  • Data accessed: the owner's channel information, the owner's uploaded video metadata, and the owner's channel analytics. No data from other users is accessed.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens and analytics numbers are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • Revoking access: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
